ZAP! Privacy Policy

ZAP! Grandpa's Channel Chaos · Last updated: 5 October 2026

In short: ZAP! never asks for your name, email or any other personal information. There are no accounts and no analytics. The game is free and shows a full-screen ad now and then between runs, from Google AdMob; a one-time purchase removes the ads for good. Your progress stays on your device. For the online leaderboards, your best scores are stored online under a random nickname such as "Cosmic Walrus". You can switch this off and remove them at any time.

What the game stores, and where

To remember your progress, ZAP! saves a small amount of game data on your device:

  • your best score in Endless, and your best score in each day's Daily Run;
  • which TV channels you have unlocked;
  • your settings for music, sound effects and vibration;
  • how many runs you have finished (to space out the ads), and whether you bought No more ads.

This data stays on your device. You can erase it at any time with Reset progress in the game's settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Online leaderboards

The game has online leaderboards: the top 100 Endless runs and the top 25 runs of each day's Daily Run. When you finish a run in Endless or the Daily Run, the game sends it to our leaderboard server, which stores:

  • a random player ID, created automatically for your copy of the game. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google or Apple account;
  • your nickname, shown publicly on the leaderboards. It is always two words from the game's own word lists, such as "Cosmic Walrus": the game gives you a random one, and you can change it by picking other words, but never by typing, so it cannot contain your real name;
  • your best score on each leaderboard, with the time it was set. Only the top runs are kept: runs that drop out of the top 100 (Endless) or top 25 (Daily Run) are deleted, and Daily Run leaderboards are deleted after about a week.

This data is used only to show the leaderboards. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never combined with other data. The leaderboard server is run on Supabase in the European Union, which stores the data on our behalf. Connections to it are encrypted.

Switching it off: in the game's settings, turn off Send my runs under Leaderboard. The game then sends nothing to the server.

Removing your data: in the game's settings, choose Remove me under Leaderboard. This permanently deletes your player ID, nickname and scores from the server. You can also ask us to delete them by email (see Contact).

Ads

ZAP! is free and shows a full-screen ad now and then between runs, never while you play. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app collects information such as your device's advertising ID, its IP address and type, and how you interact with the ads, and sends it to Google, which uses it under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this information ourselves; AdMob only shows us totals, such as how many ads were shown.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game first shows Google's consent form, where you choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time under Ad privacy in the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. The ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

No more ads: a one-time purchase removes all ads. The payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. The game only learns that No more ads was bought, and remembers it on your device.

Phone features the game uses

  • Motion sensor (accelerometer): a few channels are played by tilting the phone or keeping it still. The game reads the sensor only while you play those channels, uses the readings immediately to move things on screen, and never stores or sends them.
  • Vibration: for short buzzes when you win or lose. You can turn this off in the settings.

ZAP! does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data on your phone.

Internet and third parties

ZAP! does not need an internet connection to play. It uses the internet for the leaderboards, for the ads (Google AdMob) and for the purchase that removes them (Google Play or the App Store), all described above. It contains no analytics or tracking of its own. Apart from what Google's ad software collects, as described under Ads, no information about you is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

ZAP! is not designed for children under 13. It does not ask for any personal information, and leaderboard nicknames are made only of the game's own words.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game handles data in a new way, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
