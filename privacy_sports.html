Sports! Privacy Policy

Sports! · Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: Sports! has no analytics and no sign-up. Between matches it sometimes shows an ad from Google AdMob, which collects some data to show and measure ads (see Ads). You can buy optional outfit packs, which also turn off the ads; Google Play or the App Store handles the payment, and we never see your payment details. Your progress stays on your phone. Only if you play a friend online does the game store the name you choose, your character and your online matches on our server, so your friend can play them with you. You can delete that at any time.

What the game stores on your phone

To remember your progress, Sports! saves game data on your device:

  • your settings (sound, music, vibration) and the names of the players on this phone;
  • your character, the clothes, outfit packs and celebrations you own, your coins, your skill points and gear in each sport;
  • matches in progress against the computer or a friend on the same phone.

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app erases it.

Playing online

Playing a friend online is optional. When you use Play online, the game signs in anonymously and stores on our server:

  • a random player ID, created automatically for your copy of the game. It is not linked to your email, phone number or Google or Apple account;
  • the name you choose for online play, and your character's look and gear. The friends you play see them. Please choose a nickname, not your full name;
  • your online matches: the sport, the moves, the score and the 6-letter code you share with your friend.

Only you and the friend you play can see a match. This data is used only to let you play online. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never combined with other data. The server is run on Supabase in the European Union, which stores the data on our behalf. Connections to it are encrypted.

Matches nobody plays for 60 days, and invitations nobody joins within 14 days, are deleted automatically.

Deleting your online data: in the game's Settings, tap Delete my online data. This permanently deletes your player name, character and online matches from the server. You can also ask us to delete them by email (see Contact).

To invite a friend, the game opens your phone's share menu with the code. We do not see whom you send it to.

Outfit packs

The shop sells optional outfit packs for real money. They are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store, under their own terms and privacy policies. The store handles the payment; Sports! never sees your name, email address or payment details. The game only learns which packs your store account owns, so it can give them to you, also again after you reinstall the game or move to a new phone (tap Restore purchases in the shop). Coins are only earned by playing and cannot be bought.

Ads

Sports! shows a full-screen ad now and then as you leave a finished match against the computer or on one phone: never during a match and never in online play. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad library collects data such as your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), information about the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic information about the app. This data goes to Google, not to us; Google explains how it uses it at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you first whether ads may be personalised; you can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings (Ad privacy choices). On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google, Ads). Only ads suitable for teenagers are shown. Buying any outfit pack turns the ads off for good.

Phone features the game uses

  • Vibration: short buzzes on taps and big moments. You can turn this off in the settings.
  • Internet: for online play, ads and buying outfit packs. Everything else works without a connection.

Sports! does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data on your phone.

Third parties

Sports! has no analytics or tracking of its own. Apart from storing online play on Supabase, the ads from Google AdMob and buying outfit packs through the app store, as described above, no information about you is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

Sports! is not designed for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game handles data in a new way, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
